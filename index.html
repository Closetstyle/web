How it works

Three steps, and the first one is free.

  1. Fifteen minutes on the phone

    You send a few photos ahead of time, we talk about what's frustrating you, and you find out whether this is worth your money before you spend any.

  2. We get in the closet

    Two to three hours pulling everything out, trying things on, and building outfits from what you own. You keep photos of every look we land on.

  3. We fill the actual gaps

    Once we know what's missing, I source it — mostly secondhand, always within a budget you set first.
